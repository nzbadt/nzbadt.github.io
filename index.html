The Bridge Between Physics & Engineering

With a PhD from Brown and Postdoctoral experience at UPenn, Hebrew University and GFZ (Germany), I specialize in the mechanics of complex materials. My expertise lies at the intersection of experimental rock physics and physics-based modeling, focusing on how materials deform, fracture, and heal under coupled Thermo-Hydro-Mechanical (THM) loads.

I am transitioning to industry to apply my background in high-precision mechanical testing and predictive modeling to two primary domains:

  • Energy Transition: Ensuring the structural integrity of CCS reservoirs, hydrogen storage, and geothermal systems.
  • Defense & Aerospace: Analyzing energy dissipation, tribology, and material failure in high-performance environments.
